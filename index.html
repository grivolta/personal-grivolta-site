My principles

Four principles that don't change, whatever I'm doing.

Method can be learned and certified. What actually decides how something turns out looks a lot more like character.

01

People before the plan

A plan is worth exactly as much as the people who believe in it. So most of my time goes into listening, clearing obstacles for the people around me, and defusing conflict while it's still small.

02

Measure what matters

What actually matters gets tracked in the open, talked about honestly, and raised before it turns into a real problem. The people around me get the real picture, not the comfortable one.

03

Leave it better than you found it

Whatever I touch — a place, a relationship, an effort — should be left better than I found it. It isn't finished when it looks done: it's finished when it holds up over time.

04

Hand it on

I volunteer with the PMI Northern Italy Chapter because knowledge is a craft to be shared, not guarded. And I want the next generation — starting with my own daughter — to grow up taking people and the environment equally seriously.
