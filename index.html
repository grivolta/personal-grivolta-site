Who I am

An engineer who learned to lead people.

I started in 2011 fixing networks and firewalls for customers. Fifteen years later I lead Cloud, Data and AI programmes for large organisations, with teams of up to fifty people.

The technology changed many times along the way. What I care about stayed the same: clear plans, honest communication, and people who grow while they deliver.

Outside work I'm a husband and the dad of a three-year-old girl, so my time is precious and I spend it on purpose. I volunteer on PMI projects and keep studying, because this field rewards people who stay curious.
