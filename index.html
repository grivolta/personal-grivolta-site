How I work

Four principles that don't change, in the office or along a river.

Delivery discipline is the part that's easy to certify. What actually decides how a programme turns out looks a lot more like character.

01

People before the plan

I manage fifteen people directly and coordinate teams of fifty or more. A plan is worth exactly as much as the people who believe in it, so most of my time goes into coaching, clearing obstacles and defusing conflict early.

A team that trusts you flags the risk while it's still small.

02

Measure what matters

Scope, budget, risk, dependencies — tracked in the open, reported honestly, escalated before they do damage. Leadership gets the real picture, not the comfortable one.

It's the same instinct that makes me weigh the bags at the end of a clean-up.

03

Leave it better than you found it

A migration should leave the platform more secure and more scalable than it was. A data hub should leave a company able to trust its own numbers. Delivery isn't over when it ships — it's over when it holds.

With Plasticfree, that sentence needs no translation.

04

Hand it on

I volunteer with the PMI Northern Italy Chapter because project management is a craft to be shared, not guarded. And I want the next generation to grow up taking people and the environment equally seriously.

What you keep to yourself stops with you.
